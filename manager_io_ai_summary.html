Manager.io Rental Property Configuration

This guide outlines the complete setup and operational workflows for managing multiple rental properties using custom Control Accounts, Groups, Divisions, and imported bank statements in the latest version of Manager.io.

Core Organization Layout

To isolate property performance while keeping unified corporate statements, the system maps financial data across two distinct dimensions: rows (Chart of Accounts / Groups) and columns (Divisions).

1. Divisions (Properties)

Divisions act as property tracking codes, allowing you to filter Profit & Loss statements by individual addresses.

  • Create a unique Division for each rental property (e.g., Sawtooth, Silverbell, and General) under Settings > Divisions.
  • The "General" Division Strategy: Always create an explicit division named General / Shared for corporate expenses (like postage, CPA fees, or software). This keeps shared overhead from distorting individual property yields and prevents these lines from cluttering your audit logs.
  • Tag the Division field on every single transaction line item (invoices, payments, receipts) to ensure precise asset, liability, and expense tracking.

2. Control Accounts & Sub-Ledger Modules

Control Accounts act as summary containers on your Balance Sheet to group individual sub-accounts into clean, single-line items.

Step 1: Create the "Property Escrow Buckets" (Mortgage Escrow) Control Account (Assets)

  1. Go to Settings > Control Accounts.
  2. Select Bank and Cash Accounts
  3. Click the New Control Account button.
  4. Configure the form:
    • Name: Mortgage Escrow
    • Type: Select Asset.
  5. Click Create.

Step 2: Create and Link Real Escrow Cash Accounts

Escrow accounts hold cash belonging to you in trust. They are assets and must be created as Cash Accounts so they can receive multi-line statement file imports.

  1. Go to the Bank and Cash Accounts tab.
  2. Click New Bank or Cash Account.
  3. Build your separate property accounts: e.g.,
    • Mortgage Escrow Account - Sawtooth and Mortgage Escrow Account - Silverbell
    • Control Account: Select Mortgage Escrow.
    • Division: Select the corresponding property division (e.g., Sawtooth and Silverbell).
  4. Click Create.

3. Mortgage Loan Accounts (Standard Liabilities Group)

Mortgages are formal financial debts, not liquid cash. In Manager.io's architecture, custom Control Accounts are reserved for sub-ledger modules. Mortgages must be managed inside your standard Chart of Accounts using a sub-group.

Step 1: Create the Mortgage Loans Sub-Group

  1. Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts.
  2. Click New Group.
  3. Name it Mortgage Loans and set the Group to Liabilities. Click Create.

Step 2: Create Individual Property Loans

  1. While still in Chart of Accounts, click New Account.
  2. Build your separate property loan records (e.g., Mortgage Loan - Sawtooth and Mortgage Loan - Silverbell):
    • Group: Select your new Mortgage Loans sub-group.
    • Division: Select the corresponding property division.
    • Cash Flow Statement: Select Financing activities.
  3. Click Create.

Starting Balances

Manager.io does not use a master global system initialization date. Dates are explicitly tracked inside cash sub-ledgers, while general ledger accounts assume timeless cumulative opening values relative to your first transactional data entry point.

Go to Settings > Starting Balances to input your initial balances:

  • Escrow Balances: Click on Bank and Cash Accounts. Select your escrow account, click New Starting Balance, set your true tracking start date (e.g., 2026-01-01), and enter the opening cash amount as a Debit.
  • Mortgage Loan Balances: Click on Balance Sheet Accounts. Click New Starting Balance, select your mortgage loan account, change the type to Credit, and enter the remaining principal amount owed.
  • Property Fixed Assets: Go to Fixed Assets under the main Starting Balances menu. Click New Starting Balance. Select your asset, and enter the historical Acquisition Cost as a positive number and its cumulative prior Accumulated Depreciation as a positive number.
  • Property Land Value: Create a separate, standard asset account for Property Land Value (set Cash Flow to Investing activities). Go to Starting Balances > Balance Sheet Accounts > New Starting Balance, select the land account, and enter your land cost basis as a Debit.

Transaction Workflows & Automation

1. Property Management Statements (Net Deposits)

Property managers deposit net cash totals after collecting gross rent, taking fees, and covering repairs. You cannot match these using simple bank rules because the imported QBO text only reflects a single aggregated net cash value.

Method A: The Recurring Sales Invoice Strategy (Recommended)

  1. Enable the module under Customize Control > Recurring Sales Invoices.
  2. Create a recurring template for each property set to 1 Month(s).
  3. Include the baseline Rent Income row as a positive number and pre-tag its corresponding property Division.
  4. Monthly Processing: When statements arrive, open the pending invoice draft generated by the system. Click Edit and manually append your negative lines (Management Fees, Repairs) pre-tagging them with the same property Division.
  5. QBO Import: Import your QBO file. Go to the Uncategorized Payments/Receipts column on your dashboard, open the review screen, and apply the cash payment directly to Accounts Receivable against your customized invoice.

Method B: The Manual Split Import Strategy

  1. Import your QBO statement file.
  2. Navigate to your Bank and Cash Accounts dashboard, click the blue item tracking number inside your visible Uncategorized columns, and find the net deposit.
  3. Click Edit on that transaction to open the split-grid view.
  4. Manually split out the lines: positive for gross rent, negative for fees and maintenance.
  5. Select the matching property Division line-by-line and hit save.

2. Unpredictable Manual Transactions (The "Clone" Workflow)

Manager.io does not support dynamic form templates linked to Payee names, nor do Bank Rules process manual changes made inside a payment form UI.

  • The Workflow: To enter manual transactions with complex, repeating multi-line structures (like mortgage payments split into principal, escrow, and interest), locate your previous month’s payment under the Payments tab.
  • Click on it and select Clone at the top of the form.
  • This instantly loads a pristine payment screen with your payee, ledger accounts, and property Divisions pre-copied. Simply update the date and the current month's numerical amounts.

3. Multi-Line Escrow & Tax Transactions (The Batch Import Strategy)

Manager.io's Bank Rules engine will drop multi-line allocations into Suspense if the split values do not perfectly balance to the exact net figure imported from a QBO/CSV row. To quickly automate balanced transactions (such as a property tax payment funded out of an escrow account that has a net cash impact of \$0.00), you bypass bank rules completely and define the splits directly inside a transaction statement spreadsheet.

Step 1: Create a Multi-Line CSV Statement File

Construct a .csv spreadsheet where your balanced transactions are defined on separate lines. By maintaining identical Dates and Descriptions, Manager.io automatically merges the rows into a single multi-line transaction upon upload. 

Date,Description,Account,Division,Amount
2026-11-01,Annual Property Taxes,Property Taxes,Sawtooth,2500.00
2026-11-01,Annual Property Taxes,Mortgage Escrow Account - Sawtooth,Sawtooth,-2500.00
2026-11-01,Annual Property Taxes,Property Taxes,Silverbell,3100.00
2026-11-01,Annual Property Taxes,Mortgage Escrow Account - Silverbell,Silverbell,-3100.00

Step 2: Import & Batch Update

  1. Go to Bank and Cash Accounts and open your Escrow Clearing (Dummy) account.
  2. Click Import Bank Statement and upload your file. Match the column headers to map your accounts and divisions.
  3. Click the blue number inside your Uncategorized Payments dashboard column.
  4. Select your newly imported transaction lines, verify that the accounting rows balance to \$0.00, and click the blue Batch Update button in the bottom corner of your screen to commit them to your ledger.

Annual Straight-Line Depreciation Worksheet Automation

Manager.io's native Depreciation Calculation Worksheet uses the Declining Balance method (multiplying your rate against your shrinking book value, not the original purchase cost). To trick the worksheet into generating a flat, fixed US straight-line deduction every single year, you must adjust the percentage value annually.

The Recalculation Workflow

  1. Go to the Fixed Assets tab and find your building's current Net Book Value.
  2. Calculate your custom target worksheet percentage using this formula: 
(Required Annual Straight-Line USD Deduction / Current Net Book Value) x 100 = Target Percentage Value
  1. Click Edit on your fixed asset profile and swap your old rate for this new target percentage.
  2. Go to Reports > Depreciation Calculation Worksheet and run the current fiscal period.
  3. CRUCIAL DATE RULE: The worksheet will completely hide an asset if its starting baseline date matches the report's "From" date. Always shift the worksheet's From date to one day later than your asset start date (e.g., if asset starts 2026-01-01, set worksheet From date to 2026-01-02).
  4. Click Create Depreciation Entry at the bottom of the worksheet screen. Tag each row line item with its proper property Division (Sawtooth or Silverbell) and save to push the values to your ledger.
  5. Note: Depreciation entries never dynamically update after creation. If you edit an asset's percentage later, you must open the saved Depreciation Entry, click Edit, and manually overwrite the amount box or delete it and re-run the worksheet.

Reporting Layouts & Comparative Columns

Manager.io handles reporting matrices left-to-right from a baseline column. It cannot add up separate filtered columns horizontally to create a trailing total row. To view your properties side-by-side alongside an unallocated check and a grand total, build your columns in this precise sequence:

  1. Go to Reports > Profit and Loss Statement > New Report.
  2. Column 1 (Your Main Base Column): Title it Total Portfolio and leave the Division field completely Blank / Unselected. (This forces the system to pull your grand total company baseline first).
  3. Column 2 (Comparative): Click Add comparative column. Title it Sawtooth and select the Sawtooth division.
  4. Column 3 (Comparative): Click Add comparative column. Title it Silverbell and select the Silverbell division.
  5. Column 4 (Comparative): Click Add comparative column. Title it General Shared and select your General division.
  6. The Result: Your generated table will show your global consolidated figures in column 1, and your properties flowing perfectly next to it. Any remaining difference between Column 1 and your property columns represents any unallocated leakage.

Visual Interface & UI Management

  • Reordering the Chart of Accounts: Accounts are not locked alphabetically. To change the layout hierarchy, navigate to Settings > Chart of Accounts. You cannot drag rows on the main master sheet. Instead, click once directly on the double-ended arrow icon next to any item. This opens an isolated sub-level popup screen where you can drag and drop items vertically into your perfect layout order. Click Update to save.
  • Collapsing Groups vs. Hyperlink Access: Adding sub-groups to the Summary Page > Edit > Groups to Collapse setting hides nested lines to make your dashboard neat, but it turns the text headers gray and disables clicking on them. If you need regular access to your mortgage accounts or asset lines, remove them from the "Groups to Collapse" setting so they remain active, fully interactive blue hyperlinks.
  • Interface Scaling & Fonts: Core system fonts cannot be changed. To globally scale the text and interface sizes, hold Ctrl / Cmd and use the + or - keys to instantly zoom the responsive web wrapper layout up or down.
  • Business Logos on Reports: Manager.io natively restricts corporate logos to customer-facing documents (Invoices, Receipts). Internal compliance sheets are kept plain. To inject your brand logo automatically onto a Profit & Loss or Balance Sheet screen, click Edit on your report layout and paste a raw HTML image source tag directly into the Title text input field: 
<img src="URL_TO_YOUR_LOGO.png" style="max-height: 80px; float: right;" /> Profit and Loss Statement
