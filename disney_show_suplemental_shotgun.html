Artist Page

The Lighting Artist Page is sent to you as a link. This is how you can get to the page.

Use this as your hub and landing page in Shotgun.

Click on the Favorites star next to the "Lighting Artist Page" title.

  • This will add the page to your favorites menu:
    • All Pages > MY PAGES and you'll see the favorites.

Also, to make it very accessible:

  • Go to the artist page:
    • On the Modified dropdown (top right):
      • Set as my Home Page
  • Now you can click the F icon (used to be 'S') for Flow:
    • This will bring you to the artist page.

Notes

Notes will show up, and contain holdups and direct communication. These must be cleared to allow approval.

Keep an eye on these on your Artist Page for the most part. Dig in shots if required.

Respond to acknowledge as well as replying when complete.

Shot Page

Tasks

Click on the Tasks item (on the row of options just above the main database area and below the shot thumbnail) to see what's done and to-do on the shot.

Icon Color Status Meaning
Full Circle Green Approved Virtually Complete
Full Circle with tick Green Complete Complete for real
Half Circle Yellow In Work In-Progress (Being Worked On)
Empty Circle Tan Inventory Ready to pick up
Black Hand Blue Pre-Inventory Don't touch yet
Full Circle Purple Auxiliary Approved Ahead of downstream (needs to receive)
