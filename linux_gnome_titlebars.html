Gnome - Title Bars

Title Bars

There are at least two options for app window title bars on Gnome Desktops:

  • Roll Up/Down:
    • Double clicking reduced the window to just the title bar.
  • Min/Maximize:
    • Double clicking maximizes and minimizes (toggles) a window.

Roll Up/Down

In a terminal, run: 

gsettings set org.gnome.desktop.wm.preferences action-double-click-titlebar 'toggle-shade'

Minimize/Maximize

gsettings set org.gnome.desktop.wm.preferences action-double-click-titlebar 'toggle-shade'
