ShadowMattes - Catching Shadows in Hyperion

Activate ShadowMattes

This is a bit hidden. It's in the HyperionSettings node. You have to activate them to ALLOW them as an option.

  • Go to the 'HyperionSettings' node in the pass network (where things like isMatte, hiddenFromCamera etc. live).
  • On the 'Render' tab, activate 'Render Outputs'
  • On the tri-color icon on the right of the field, click:
    • for the light emission, click on the [..] and choose 'shadowMatte'

Set for the Emission/Light

In the Render Outputs Edit node (usually just below the HyperionSettings node):

  • At the end of the 'Add' field, click on the sharp white arrow icon
  • Click on the [..] for the light(s) that need the shadowMatte
  • Select ShadowMatte

dpip Modifier

  • Switch off all emissions except the shadow light(s):
    • Targets:
      • :wdas:shot:lrig*:trs_master
      • Excludes:
        • Your light(s)
