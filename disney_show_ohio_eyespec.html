Eye Specs

Specs

  • Both eyes should have specs.
  • Make sure spec is from direction of the key or main light.
  • Remove distracting reflections.
  • Eye specs should be subtle over sclera/eye whites.
  • Position is ideally on the border of the pupil and the iris.
  • You can tint the spec with key light temperature if needed.

Caustics

  • Both eyes should have caustics.
  • Caustics are always 180deg from the spec.
  • Keep iris color all around.

IBL images can be used if complex scene data needs to be shown in the reflections.

Two Approaches to Eye Specs:

  • Physical - Houdini:
    • From the lighting rig
    • Preferred
  • CG - Comp/Nuke:
    • Sticks the eyespec artificially
    • Compromised
    • Useful if character moves to various key positions

Create Eye Spec Pass (natural)

  • Click on RenderPassManager (at network base of your 'packet' or 'shot'):
    • In GUI:
      • Template -> eyeSpec
      • Category -> CHARACTER
      • ch_all_eyeSpec_ch_all_meniscus (auto-populated)
NOTE: There's an eyeSpecProjection template here.
NOTE: There's a 'meniscus' template here too.

The template will produce a render pass.

  • It disables the cornea bulge (subnetwork node).
  • It prunes the environment, fx and leaves the characters. (lighten_scene):
    • There are Target Methods in there with Primitive Pattern fields.
  • A TMOD to boost caustics. (transportmod_boost_eyeSpec_caustics)
  • An EyeSpec node

EyeSpec Node

This is for single characters.

In the node's parm panel:

  • Character:
    • Click on dropdown arrow at the end of the field.
    • Choose one of the scene's characters that should be listed here.
    • Scene will cook.
  • Camera:
    • Check that the shot cam is the correct one:
      • /shot/shotCam/CAM or whatever - the spec is camera-direction-dependent.

Two switches for the viewport:

  • Switch to Ion
  • Switch to Chimera

Use Chimera for placing the specs, for speed with enough information to place elements.

At this stage, you should have the elements that drive the eyespecs.

Placement

When you change values on this tab, hit Place Lights button.

  • Sync L to R and Sync R to L:
    • copy values to 'other' eye.

There are controls in the Placement tab of the EyeSpec node.

  • Sticky:
    • 0 - physically correct.
    • 1 - fully sticky to the eyes.
  • O'Clock:
    • Values here represent 'hours' of a clock (for direction)
  • Pullback:
    • Size.
    • Larger is smaller.
    • default is 22.
  • Distance:
    • Distance from eye geo center.
    • 10 could be good.
  • Roll:
    • Works most effectively on 'mapped' spec lights (with texture).
    • Rotates on z axis.
  • Handle Size:
    • Control the viewport representation of handles and controls.

Zooming in to see angles

Switch to Chimera in the Viewport.

This is done on the PanAndZoom2D node:

  • IMPORTANT: Switch to the utility camera:
    • cameras/dpipe_PanAndZoom3D
  • dpipe_PanAndZoom2d:
    • Click on the node, and in the parm panel:
      • Zoom (multiplier):
      • horizontal offset (%)
      • vertical offset (%)

In the Viewport, choose the correct Viewport switches to see the actual lights.

Then look through Ion to get the final position.

Specular Properties Tab

Adjust the lights:

  • Exposure - 6.5 is a good value
  • Light Color
  • Emission Category - default of 1 (rendered separately, so shouldn't clash)
  • Radius - 10 is a good value
  • Cucoloris map:
    • Click on arrow at end of the field to choose one of 3 maps
    • IBL maps
  • etc.

Render

Context Options Editor

Choose your eyespec channel to test the render in Helios.

You should see the whole eyes with caustics and spec. If you set it up correctly, you could also get an IBL as the rgb layer.

Caustics

  • Rotation L:
    • move the lighting around
  • Rotation R:
    • move the lighting around
  • Scale:
    • How far the brightness spreads
    • 0.3 is good
  • Caustic Softness:
    • How soft the transitions are
    • 0.4 is good
  • Gain:
    • Value of light
    • Tint of light
  • Erode Iris Matte Size:
    • Eat into or expand edge of iris
    • Plus or minus

General IBL Properties

  • IBL Mode:
    • Sticky:
      • Lock on eyes
    • Physical:
      • natural based on shader
