Recurring Deduction Prompts

To set up an automated year-end reminder in Manager.io so it prompts you every December 31 to post your amortization write-offs etc, use the Recurring Journal Entries feature.

When a recurring schedule hits its due date, Manager.io displays a yellow notification banner at the top of your Journal Entries tab so you can post it in one click.

Create the Annual Amortization Schedule

  1. Go to Settings on your left sidebar menu
  2. Click Recurring Transactions
  3. Click Recurring Journal Entries
  4. New Recurring Journal Entry.
  5. Configure the schedule settings:
    • Next issue date: Set to 2026-12-31.
    • Interval: Select Every 12 Months
    • End date: Set to 2040-12-31 (or leave blank and delete it after the final year).
    • Narration: Annual Loan Cost Amortization - [Property Name]:
      • or to exploit the tip below:
        • Annual Loan Cost Amortization - [Property Name] (REMINDER: Also clone Depreciation Entry!)
  6. Activate the Column - Decription checkbox.
  7. Build the 2-line grid below:
    Line Account Description Debit Credit Division
    1 Loan Cost Amortization Expense Annual 30-yr loan cost tax deduction 69.68   [Property Name]
    2 Unamortized Loan Costs - [Property Name] Reduces remaining unamortized asset balance   69.68 [Property Name]
  8. Click Create.

How the Prompt Works Each Year

  • On December 31: A yellow banner reading 1 recurring journal entry due will appear at the top of your Journal Entries tab (and in your left sidebar counter).
  • One-Click Posting: Click the banner, and verify the date (YYYY-12-31)
  • Click Create.
  • Manager.io immediately posts the deduction and automatically advances the next reminder to December 31 of the following year.
    Tip: Add a reminder to the Narration field, to remind you to clone the annual Depreciation Entry for the property.
