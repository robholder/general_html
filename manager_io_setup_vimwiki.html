Overview

This guide covers the step-by-step setup procedure for Manager.io business files based on the video playlist (above) transcript notes.

Setup Steps by Video

Video 1: Setup a New Business

  • Download & Install:
    • Visit manager.io and download the free desktop software for Windows, Mac, or Linux.
  • Create Business:
    • Launch Manager.io
    • Click Add a Business, and
    • Select Create New Business.
  • Enter Details:
    • Input your business name (e.g., Active Ear Solutions) and
    • Select your country (e.g., United States).
  • Save:
    • Click Create a new business.
  • Folder Location (Optional):
    • Note or adjust the storage location in your local Documents folder.

Video 2: Customizing and Renaming a Business

  • Rename Business :: Open the business file, click Rename next to the business name, enter the new name (e.g., ABC Corp), and click Rename.
  • Edit Business Details :: Go to Settings to add address information, configure base settings, set date formats, and select your chart of accounts.
  • Extensions :: (Optional) Add functional extensions directly from Manager.io's site via Settings.

Video 3: How to Remove a Business

  • Access Tab :: Click on the Businesses tab.
  • Select Remove :: Click Remove Business.
  • Confirm :: Choose the target business from the drop-down menu and confirm removal.

Video 4: How to Set the Base Currency

  • Navigate to Currency Settings :: Go to Settings > Currencies > Base Currency.
  • Input Details :: Enter your country's currency code (e.g., USD), currency name (e.g., US Dollar), symbol ($), and specify decimal places (e.g., 2).
  • Save :: Click Update to apply the base currency.

Video 5: How to Add Foreign Currencies

  • Open Foreign Currencies :: Go to Settings > Currencies > Foreign Currencies.
  • Add Currency :: Click New Foreign Currency.
  • Configure Fields :: Fill in the Currency Code (e.g., CNY or EUR), Name, Symbol, and decimal places.
  • Save :: Click Create to store the foreign currency.

Video 6: Setup Right-to-Left (RTL) Languages

  • Open Language Options :: Go to the main Summary dashboard and scroll down to the bottom footer.
  • Select Language :: Click the English tab and select an RTL language (e.g., Hebrew, Arabic, Farsi, or Kurdish).
  • Revert :: Click the language button again and re-select English to switch back to left-to-right reading.

Video 7: Set the Date and Number Format

  • Access Settings :: Go to Settings > Date & Number Format.
  • Date & Time :: Choose your preferred date pattern (e.g., Month/Day/Year) and time format (12-hour AM/PM vs. 24-hour).
  • First Day of Week :: Set the starting day of your week (e.g., Sunday).
  • Number Format :: Select your preferred separator style for thousands and decimals (e.g., 1,000.00).
  • Save :: Click Update.

Video 8: Backup, Restore, Transfer, and Import Business

  • Backing Up :: Navigate to the Businesses tab, choose the business, click Backup, choose whether to include attachments/history, and select a local destination folder.
  • Importing/Restoring :: On your new installation, go to the Businesses tab, click Add Business > Import Business, locate the backup file, and click Import.

Video 9: Adding a Bank Account, Owner's Equity, and Owner's Contribution

  • Enable Sidebar Tabs :: Click Customize, check Bank & Cash Accounts, Receipts, and Capital Accounts, then click Update.
  • Create Bank Account :: Go to Bank & Cash Accounts > New Bank Account and enter the name (e.g., Sally's Checking Account).
  • Add Owner :: Go to Capital Accounts and add the owner's name (e.g., Sally).
  • Rename Equity Section :: Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts, click Edit under Capital Accounts, rename it to Owner's Equity, and click Update.
  • Record Contribution :: Go to Receipts > New Receipt, set the destination as the checking account, set the account line to Owner's Equity (sub-account Funds contributed under the owner's capital account), enter the contributed sum, and click Create.

Video 10: Customizing the Chart of Accounts and Control Accounts

  • Modify Existing Accounts :: Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts; rename unused default accounts (e.g., change Donations to Office Supplies) or delete unneeded zero-transaction accounts.
  • Create Custom Groups :: Click New Group, name it (e.g., Cost of Goods Sold), assign it to Expenses, and use the up/down arrows to position it above general expenses.
  • Nest Sub-Accounts :: Create new expense accounts (e.g., Floral Inventory, Floral Supplies, Freight In) and place them inside your custom group.
  • Create Individual Control Accounts :: Go to Settings > Control Accounts > Bank & Cash Accounts; create dedicated asset/liability control accounts for specific checking, savings, or credit card accounts.
  • Link Accounts :: Edit each account in Bank & Cash Accounts to map it to its specific control account so balances display cleanly on the Summary screen.

Video 11: Setting Up Owner & Equity Account, Bank & Credit Card Account

  • Activate Required Tabs :: Enable Bank & Cash Accounts, Receipts, and Capital Accounts under Customize.
  • Create Credit Card Control Account :: Go to Settings > Control Accounts > Bank & Cash Accounts > New Control Account, title it Credit Cards, and assign its classification to Liabilities.
  • Add Credit Card Account :: Go to Bank & Cash Accounts > New Banker Cash Account, enter the card name (e.g., Sally's Visa), and set the control account to Credit Cards.
  • Enter Opening Balance :: Go to Settings > Starting Balances > Bank & Cash Accounts, pick the credit card account, and input a negative amount (e.g., -$50) to accurately reflect pre-existing debts owed.

Video 12: Setting Up Credit Card Accounts

  • Create Control Account :: Go to Settings > Control Accounts > Bank & Cash Accounts, create a Credit Cards account, and set it as a Liability.
  • Add Card Account :: Create a new cash/bank account for the credit card and map it to the Credit Cards liability control account.
  • Set Negative Balance :: Go to Settings > Starting Balances > Bank & Cash Accounts and enter a negative value for pre-existing debts (entering a positive number creates a false overpayment credit).

Video 13: Setting Up Suppliers, Payments, and Digital Receipts

  • Enable Suppliers Tab :: Go to Customize, check Suppliers, and click Update.
  • Add Supplier :: Navigate to Suppliers > New Supplier, input the business name (e.g., Walmart), and click Create.
  • Record Payment :: Go to Payments > New Payment, select the source payment account (e.g., credit card), choose the supplier, select the expense account (e.g., Office Supplies), input the payment amount, and click Create.
  • Attach Digital Receipt :: Open the payment record, click Choose File, select a stored receipt image/document, and attach it directly to the digital entry.
