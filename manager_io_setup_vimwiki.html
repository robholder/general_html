Video 8: Backup, Restore, Transfer, and Import Business

External YouTube Link - Video 8

  • Backing Up: Navigate to the Businesses tab,
    • Choose the business,
    • Click Backup,
    • This next part is not correct:
    • Choose whether to include attachments/history, and select a local destination folder.
    • You actually get:
      • Timestamp
      • Attachments

1. Timestamp

  • What it means: When checked, Manager.io automatically adds the exact date and time to the end of your backup file name (e.g., YourBusinessName - 2026-10-31.manager).
  • Why it matters: It prevents you from accidentally overwriting your older backups. If you back up your files every Friday, keeping this checked creates a historical timeline of your data (e.g., Week 1, Week 2, Week 3) instead of continuously replacing a single file named YourBusinessName.manager.
  • The Verdict: Always leave this checked. It is your best insurance policy against accidental data loss.

2. Attachments

  • What it means: This controls whether the backup file will include any external documents you have uploaded or dragged-and-dropped into Manager.io. This includes things like PDF supplier invoices, scanned receipts, or signed customer contracts attached to specific transactions.
  • Why it matters: Manager.io stores attachments in a separate subfolder on your computer to keep the core accounting ledger running fast. Checking this box forces the app to bundle all of those heavy PDF and image files directly into the backup file.
  • The Verdict: Leave this checked for your primary historical backups. Note that including attachments will make your backup file significantly larger, which means it might take a few extra seconds to upload to cloud storage or a thumb drive. If you only care about the financial data (the numbers) and already have your PDFs saved safely elsewhere on your Mac, you can uncheck it to keep the file size tiny.

Video 10: Customizing the Chart of Accounts and Control Accounts

External YouTube Link - Video 10

  • Modify Existing Accounts:
    • Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts;
    • The next item is no longer relevant, as the profit and loss section is no longer pre-configured:
    • Rename unused default accounts (e.g., change Donations to Office Supplies) or delete unneeded zero-transaction accounts.

To get those categories to appear on your summary dashboard, you need to build the structure yourself. You can do this in two ways:

Method A: The Clean Way (Add Groups & Accounts)

  • Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts.
  • On the right side of the screen (the Profit & Loss Statement side), click New Group.
    • Name it Income, ensure the
    • Type is set to Income group, and
    • Click Create.
  • Click New Group again.
    • Name it Expenses, set the
    • Type to Expense group, and click
    • Create.
  • Now click New Account.
    • Name it something like Sales Revenue,
    • Set the group to Income, and
    • Click Create.

Method B: The Automatic Way (Activate Tabs)

Manager.io uses an "on-demand" module setup. The moment you activate certain operational tabs on the left sidebar, the software will automatically generate the required hidden P&L tracking lines for you: • Enabling Customers + Sales Invoices auto-generates Sales lines. • Enabling Inventory Items auto-generates Inventory - sales and Inventory - cost.

  • Create Custom Groups:
    • Click New Group,
    • Name it (e.g., Cost of Goods Sold),
    • Assign it to Expenses, and use the up/down arrows to position it above general expenses.
  • Nest Sub-Accounts:
    • Create new expense accounts (e.g., Floral Inventory, Floral Supplies, Freight In) and place them inside your custom group.
  • Create Individual Control Accounts:
    • Go to Settings > Control Accounts > Bank & Cash Accounts;
    • Create dedicated asset/liability control accounts for specific checking, savings, or credit card accounts.
  • Link Accounts:
    • Edit each account in Bank & Cash Accounts to map it to its specific control account so balances display cleanly on the Summary screen.

Video 11: Setting Up Owner & Equity Account, Bank & Credit Card Account

External YouTube Link - Video 11

  • Activate Required Tabs:
    • Enable Bank & Cash Accounts, Receipts, and Capital Accounts under Customize.
  • Create Credit Card Control Account:
    • Go to Settings > Control Accounts > Bank & Cash Accounts > New Control Account,
    • Title it Credit Cards, and assign its classification to Liabilities.
  • Add Credit Card Account:
    • Go to Bank & Cash Accounts > New Banker Cash Account,
    • Enter the card name (e.g., Sally's Visa), and set the control account to Credit Cards.
  • Enter Opening Balance:
    • Go to Settings > Starting Balances > Bank & Cash Accounts,
    • Pick the credit card account, and input a negative amount (e.g., -$50) to accurately reflect pre-existing debts owed.
