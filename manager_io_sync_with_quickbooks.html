Manager.io Mid-Year Cutover Checklist (Sept 30, 2026 Snapshot)

Goal: Parallel-run Oct 1 – Dec 31, 2026 in Manager.io alongside QuickBooks, with complete full-year 2026 Cash-basis reports ready for clean Jan 1, 2027 switchover.

Phase 1: QuickBooks Export (Per Business File)

  • Set QuickBooks reporting basis to Cash Basis.
  • Export Balance Sheet as of 2026-09-30 (reconciled bank balances, fixed assets, accumulated depreciation, loans, security deposits, partner capital & YTD draws).
  • Export Profit & Loss for 2026-01-01 to 2026-09-30 (broken out by Property/Class for the 2-property and 3-property files).

Phase 2: Manager.io Structure & Starting Balances (As of 2026-09-30)

  • Multi-Property Files Only: Create one Division per property under Settings -> Divisions before entering balances.
  • Partnership Files Only: Create separate Equity accounts for each partner:
    • Partner [Name] - Capital (Credit balance - CLIC)
    • Partner [Name] - Drawings (Debit balance - Contra-Equity / DEAD)
  • Enter Starting Balances as of 2026-09-30 for Balance Sheet accounts only:
    • Assets (Debits - DEAD): Checking/Savings reconciled balances, Property/Building/Equipment original cost.
    • Contra-Assets (Credits): Accumulated Depreciation as of Sept 30 (displays in parentheses under Assets).
    • Liabilities (Credits - CLIC): Mortgage/Loan principal balances, Tenant Security Deposits held.
    • Equity (Credits/Debits): Partner Capital (Credit), Partner YTD Drawings (Debit), Prior Retained Earnings (Credit).

Phase 3: Jan 1 – Sept 30 Cash P&L Snapshot Journal Entry

  • Create one Journal Entry dated 2026-09-30 per business file:
    • Credit each Cash Income account (Rental Income, etc.) for Jan 1 – Sept 30 totals (tag Division per property).
    • Debit each Cash Expense account (Repairs, Taxes, Insurance, Interest, Depreciation, etc.) for Jan 1 – Sept 30 totals (tag Division per property).
    • Offset Line: Balance the net Jan–Sep profit/loss to Retained Earnings (or split across Partner Equity accounts if Sept 30 Balance Sheet already includes YTD profit):
      • Net Profit Jan–Sep -> Debit Retained Earnings
      • Net Loss Jan–Sep -> Credit Retained Earnings

Phase 4: Penny-for-Penny Verification (Before Oct–Dec Transactions)

  • Run Manager.io Balance Sheet (Cash basis, as of 2026-09-30) -> Confirm exact match to QuickBooks Sept 30 Balance Sheet (and Partner balances).
  • Run Manager.io Profit & Loss (Cash basis, 2026-01-01 to 2026-09-30, broken out by Division) -> Confirm exact match to QuickBooks Jan–Sep P&L.
  • Import/enter Oct 1 – Dec 31, 2026 bank transactions (tagged by Division in multi-property files) and compare full-year 2026 reports against QuickBooks at year-end.

Explanation of Retained Earnings Step

The Retained Earnings step serves both to mathematically balance the Journal Entry (Debits = Credits) AND to prevent your Jan 1–Sept 30 profit from being counted twice on your Balance Sheet.

Here is why both jobs happen at the exact same time:

1. Mathematical Job: Every Journal Entry Must Balance (Debits = Credits)

  • When you enter your Jan 1–Sept 30 Income (Credits, e.g., ＄16,200) and Expenses (Debits, e.g., ＄6,200), those two sides won't equal each other unless your business made exactly ＄0.00 profit.
  • The ＄10,000 gap between Income and Expenses is your Net Profit, so you need a ＄10,000 Debit line to make Total Debits (＄16,200) = Total Credits (＄16,200).

2. Accounting Job: Avoiding Double-Counting on the Balance Sheet

  • In Manager.io, every Profit & Loss account automatically flows into Retained Earnings on your Balance Sheet.
  • Because your Sept 30 Checking account and Sept 30 Balance Sheet starting balances already contain that ＄10,000 cash profit you earned between January and September, putting ＄10,000 of net profit onto the P&L would otherwise push Retained Earnings up by an extra ＄10,000 that doesn't exist.
  • Debiting Retained Earnings (＄10,000) on that same 2026-09-30 Journal Entry cancels out that automatic Profit & Loss push - leaving your Balance Sheet untouched while your Profit & Loss report now displays the full Jan–Sep income and expense breakdown!

Note: If you entered your Sept 30 Balance Sheet starting balances using Dec 31, 2025 Retained Earnings rather than Sept 30 Retained Earnings, then you actually want the ＄10,000 Jan–Sep profit to flow into Retained Earnings - in which case the Debit offset is simply what makes your Sept 30 Balance Sheet balance in the first place!
