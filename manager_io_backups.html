Manager.io - Backups

The In-App Solution (Manual, Single-Business)

Backup a Business

Manager.io has a built-in backup button, though it only backs up one business at a time and must be triggered manually:

  • How it works: Open a business, click the Backup button at the top right of the accounting interface.
  • What it produces: It generates a self-contained, compressed file named YourBusinessName (YYYY-MM-DD).manager.
  • Limitation: There is no built-in scheduler or "Backup All Businesses" button.

Restore a Backup/Business

How to Restore a Backup in Manager.io

  1. Open Manager.io and navigate to the main Businesses screen (click Businesses in the top-left corner if you are currently inside a company file).
  2. Click the Add Business button.
  3. Select Import Business from the dropdown menu.
  4. Click Choose File, locate your saved YourBusinessName (YYYY-MM-DD).manager backup file, and click Import Business (or Cancel).

Important Detail to Know When Restoring

  • It Creates a Copy (Does Not Overwrite): Importing a backup adds it as a separate business on your main Businesses list. If your original business is still listed there, you will temporarily see two businesses with the same name. Once you open the restored version and verify that all your numbers look right, you can safely delete the old or corrupted entry from the Businesses screen.

Deleting a Business

On the home screen (Businesses):

  • Click top Remove Business
  • Choose the Business to delete from the dropdown menu.
  • Click the dialog Remove Business button (or Cancel).
