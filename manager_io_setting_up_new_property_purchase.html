Record a New Property Purchase (Closing Journal Entry)

Overview

When placing a new rental property into service, record the entire Closing Disclosure (CD) / HUD-1 settlement statement in a single balanced Journal Entry dated on the official closing date.

  • Building vs. Land Split: Only the building structure can be depreciated; land must sit in its own separate Fixed Asset account (commonly split using your county tax assessor's land-to-improvement ratio).
  • Earnest Money & Down Payment: Any cash wired to escrow before or at closing is credited out of your Checking account (or Partner Capital if paid personally by an owner).

Step 1: Verify Required Accounts

Before creating the entry, confirm these accounts exist in Settings > Chart of Accounts (or Fixed Assets / Control Accounts):

  • Assets (Balance Sheet):
    • Land - [Property Name] (Fixed Asset)
    • Building - [Property Name] (Fixed Asset)
    • Unamortized Loan Costs - [Property Name] (under Financing & Borrowing Costs)
    • Mortgage Escrow - [Property Name] (Bank/Control Account for prepaid property tax/insurance reserves)
  • Liabilities (Balance Sheet):
    • Mortgage Principal - [Property Name] (Liability)
  • Expenses (Profit & Loss):
    • Mortgage Interest (for prepaid daily interest from closing date to month-end)
    • Property Taxes / Insurance (if prorated or paid directly at closing rather than held in escrow)

Step 2: Enter the Closing Journal Entry

Go to Journal Entries > New Journal Entry:

  1. Date: Set to the official closing date (YYYY-MM-DD).
  2. Narration: Property Purchase Closing Settlement - [Property Name]
  3. Check Column - Description below the line items and populate the closing lines (adjusting rows to match your specific Closing Disclosure):
    Line Account Description Debit Credit Division
    1 Building - [Property Name] Depreciable structure + capitalized closing fees [Building $]   [Property Name]
    2 Land - [Property Name] Non-depreciable land allocation [Land $]   [Property Name]
    3 Unamortized Loan Costs - [Property Name] Loan origination, points, appraisal, lender fees [Loan Fee $]   [Property Name]
    4 Mortgage Escrow - [Property Name] Initial tax & insurance escrow reserve deposits [Escrow $]   [Property Name]
    5 Mortgage Interest Prepaid daily interest at closing [Interest $]   [Property Name]
    6 Mortgage Principal - [Property Name] New mortgage loan principal balance   [Loan Amt $] [Property Name]
    7 Checking Account (or Capital Account) Earnest money + final cash-to-close wired   [Cash Out $] [Property Name]
  4. Confirm Total Debits = Total Credits and click Create.

Step 3: Set Up Ongoing Depreciation

Once the purchase entry is saved:

  • Divide Building - [Property Name] cost basis by 27.5 years (residential US rental) to determine your full-year straight-line depreciation, prorating Year 1 using the IRS mid-month convention based on the closing month.
  • Record annual depreciation alongside your Loan Cost Amortization at year-end (YYYY-12-31).
