Reading and Configuring the Manager.io Summary Screen

The Summary dashboard provides a real-time, double-entry financial overview of your rental property business. It is divided into two columns that operate on entirely different time horizons: the permanent balance sheet on the left and the temporary operational ledger on the right.

1. The Core Architecture & Rental Property Mapping

Left Side: Cumulative Balances (The Life of the Account)

The accounts under Assets, Liabilities, and Equity represent a continuous, unbroken timeline from Day 1. These numbers never reset to zero at the end of a tax year.

  • *Assets:* Everything of value that your rental business owns. For your portfolio, this includes liquid checking balances, your dedicated property escrow cash accounts (Mortgage Escrow Account - Sawtooth), your non-depreciable raw land values, and the remaining paper value of your buildings (Original Acquisition Cost minus past Accumulated Depreciation).
  • *Liabilities:* Third-party financial debts your business is obligated to repay. This displays the remaining principal balances owed on your property mortgage loans (Mortgage Loan - Sawtooth).
  • *Equity:* The net "book worth" belonging to you as the owner, mathematically calculated as Assets - Liabilities. It displays your personal ownership stake via the *Capital Accounts* module (your cash injections) combined with your cumulative historical *Retained Earnings* (your lifetime profits or losses).

Right Side: Operational Performance (The Active Window)

The accounts under Income, Expenses, and Net Profit/Loss measure speed and cash flow performance. These lines are designed to capture a temporary, restricted slice of time.

  • *Less: Expenses:* The active operational burden required to maintain your portfolio during the selected time period. At initial setup, this shows a negative net balance driven entirely by your current year's *Depreciation Entries*. Because depreciation is an active annual operational expense, it registers here even if no physical cash has left your bank.
  • *Net Profit (Loss):* Calculated as Income - Expenses. At setup, with your depreciation expenses logged but no tenant rent yet imported, this will display a temporary negative loss. This is perfectly normal and will begin to turn positive as you import active rental statement revenues.

2. Managing the Summary Time Period Parameters

By default, Manager.io displays a timeless cumulative view for the Balance Sheet while automatically showing a rolling 12-month window or the current financial year for the Profit & Loss column. When a new fiscal year is crossed, the income and expense lines reset back to $0.00, sweeping the old year's totals permanently into your historical Retained Earnings bucket.

To adjust, override, or lock the active reporting period displayed on your dashboard, use the exact sequence below:

Sequence to Customize the Dashboard Date Range

  1. Navigate to the *Summary* tab on your left sidebar menu.
  2. Click the *Edit* button located at the very top of the main summary page view.
  3. Locate the parameter options and check the box labeled *"Show balances for specified period"* (this is turned off by default, which causes the page to display its standard rolling/annual mix).
  4. Enter your exact target parameters into the *From* and *To* calendar date input fields:
    • To view a single tax year's performance, set the dates from January 1st to December 31st.
    • To view the cumulative lifetime income and expense totals for your entire account history, set the "From" date to your original migration launch date and leave the "To" date on the current day.
  5. Click *Update* at the bottom of the form to commit your layout rules.
