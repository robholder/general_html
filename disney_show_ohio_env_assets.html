Switching variants

To switch variants:

  • Drop a setVariant node:
    • Drag in asset to Primitives:
    • Click on the arrow at end of the Variant Set field
      • Choose assetVariants
    • In the Variant Name field, click the arrow:
      • Choose one of the available assetVariants:
        • e.g., varSimpleLightFog

Rendering:

  • Choose each variant for different passes (light & fog)
