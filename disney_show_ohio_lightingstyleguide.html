Overall Style

  • Brighter
  • Crisper (contrast/less atmosphere)
  • More Colorful
  • Maximize appeal

Atmo

There'll be less atmospherics in this show.

Avoid Aim
Flattening Motivated
Desatruation Specific
- Color in Atmosphere if present

Counter Measures to Lack of Atmosphere

  • Use shafts and pools of light

Key/Fill Ratios

  • Not too much fill.
  • Keep contrast and punch

Chromatic Aberration

  • Only in 'magic' scenes

Bloom

  • Keep photographic
  • Not fuzzy
  • Don't pollute colors

Character Lighting

Skin and Specular

  • Feel the specular on skin
  • Avoid dry looking flesh
  • But not crazy bright (for DCI/Color Timing)

Rims

  • Only one dominant side
  • Avoid 'cut out' look

Eye Whites

  • Sclera should not be too bright
  • Have shaping

Eye Specs

  • Crescent shaped (especially hero poses)
  • Match angle of iris (not sure what this means)

Irises

  • Violet in color
  • Not brown

"11" Lines in Mid Brow

  • Keep subtle
  • Mattes exist to masage

Eye Shadowing

  • Flatten and brighten eye orbits

Mouth/Face Expression Shadows

  • Minimize shading on expression shapes
  • Keep shadow shapes soft
  • Faces are one area that can use more fill

Hair

  • Avoid dusty looking appearance
  • Avoid overly bright specular hits and blowout
  • Keep accent hair colors
  • Keep detail in the bangs (side dangles)
  • Tips of hair can get too bright:
    • Soften the extreme values to make more subtle

Bang Shadows

  • Reduce shadow intensity from bangs onto face

Jaw & Neck

  • Avoid too strong shadowing on the neck from the jaw
  • Keep color in shadows warm:
    • Example: Top of shadow (next to jaw) warm, and moving down to fill color
  • Low contrast between jaw and neck (shadow)
  • Don't emphasize jawline

Inside Mouths

  • Avoid darks and grays:
    • Warmer is better
    • Not like a void

Color Relationships

  • Maintain color ratios while lighting:
    • Example: White shirt and other character tones.

Magic on Skin

  • Magical light should appear to emanate from within skin (subsurface)

Trees

  • Keep strong shaping
  • Keep shaping saturated (not gray shadowing)
  • Model branches too

Aspect Ratios

Human World

  • 1920 x 804
  • Aspect ratio of 2:39

Witch World

  • 1920 x 1038
  • Aspect ration of 1:85
