The Difference Between Inter-Account Transfers & Journal Entries

In Manager.io, the core difference is that an Inter-Account Transfer deals strictly with physical liquid cash assets, while a Journal Entry is used for non-cash accounting adjustments.

Because Manager.io keeps its cash ledgers highly locked down to protect your bank reconciliation reports, the software treats these two screens entirely differently:

1. Inter-Account Transfer (Moving Real Cash)

You use this tab exclusively when you are moving real, liquid money directly from one of your Bank/Cash/Escrow accounts to another Bank/Cash/Escrow account within your business.

  • How it handles your ledger: It updates your cash sub-ledgers on both sides instantly. It simultaneously tells the first bank ledger that money went out (a credit) and tells the second bank ledger that money came in (a debit).
  • Rental Examples:
    • You transfer $2,000 from your main Sawtooth Operating Checking account over to your Silverbell Checking account to cover a repair bill.
    • You move cash from your primary property business checking account into your Property Escrow Bucket cash account.

2. Journal Entry (Moving Abstract Values)

You use this tab strictly to shift financial balances between standard accounts where no cash is changing hands.

  • The Strict Banking Rule: Manager.io completely blocks you from selecting any Bank, Cash, or Escrow accounts inside a standard Journal Entry form. If cash is involved, you are forbidden from using this tab.
  • Rental Examples:
    • You calculate your annual building depreciation and want to move a portion of your asset value into your expense sheets.
    • You apply a tenant’s Security Deposit (a liability) to wipe out a balance of Unpaid Rent (an income asset) after they move out. No physical dollars moved, but the financial values shifted.

Direct Comparison Summary

Feature / Action Inter-Account Transfer Journal Entry
Are Bank/Cash/Escrow accounts allowed? Yes. (In fact, both sides must be cash accounts). No. (The system completely locks them out).
Does it affect your bank reconciliations? Yes. It creates matching entries ready to clear your statement imports. No. It has zero impact on your liquid bank records.
What does it move? Physical, liquid capital. Abstract values, debts, equity, and asset write-offs.
