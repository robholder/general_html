Notes

In Houdini you can create lights by:

  • Tab and dpipe followed by name/type of light
  • Shelf tool Light and Materials Shortcuts
  • Within Light Box node

Look Through Light

  • Viewport:
    • Right side lozenge
    • Look Through Light, and choose
    • Lock the icon on the Right Vertical panel

Tip: Select character (target), the to frame on it:

  • Unlock
  • <Space> f
  • Lock

Snap Tool

  • select light
  • Click magnet icon on Left Vertical panel
  • Move mouse to 'snap' to geo in the viewport

Look At Mode

Add extra manipulators to a selected light:

  • <Shift> + L

Then use the magnet icon (LVP) to snap the target manipulator on the light to geometry

Numerical Settings

On the light's Transform tab, you can adjust conventionally with numbers.

  • MMB on a field to envoke Houdini adjustments in number fields

You can append values in Houdini's numerical fields

  • Transform tab > Operator Order > Append

Types of Lights

  • Distant light
  • Dome light
  • Quad Light
  • Sphere light
  • Spot light

Parm Panel

  • Palette Prim:
    • Choose where to put the light.
  • Name - NOT THE NODE NAME IN THE INTERFACE:
    • (Rename an item iwth the top field title.)
  • Template:
    • Choose light time after creation (change).
    • You'll lose information if you switch after editing.
  • Hyperedit button:
    • Update 'this' light in Helios.

Tabs

Emission

  • Emissive: On/Off toggle
  • Emission Category - dropdown menu:
    • Asterisk will indicate that the emission number is already used.
    • However, you can add a light to an existing category number.
