Ingesting CSV or QBO Files

1. How to Ingest a QBO or CSV File into Manager.io

Before you can allocate transactions, you must load the data file into your designated checking ledger:

  1. Click on the Bank and Cash Accounts tab on your left sidebar menu.
  2. Look at the bottom of the pane, somewhere below the listed accounts.
  3. Click the Import Bank Statement button.
  4. Select the target account from the dropdown menu.
  5. Click Choose File
  6. Select your downloaded .qbo or .csv file
  7. Click Next.
  8. Review the data staging screen. Manager.io will show you a preview list of all the imported dates and amounts.
  9. Scroll to the bottom and click Import.

Your newly ingested QBO transactions are currently sitting in two separate places. Here is exactly how to find and access them right now:

Path A: Check Active Columns

Activating the Relevant Columns

Here is the quick-reference guide to activate the correct visibility columns on your bank account screen, formatted directly for your notes:

How to Activate Uncategorized Columns in Manager.io

  1. Navigate to the Bank and Cash Accounts tab on the left sidebar menu.
  2. Click the Edit columns button located at the very bottom right of the page grid.
  3. Review the available parameter list and check the boxes for the following required tracking columns:
    • Uncategorized payments (Isolates your incoming QBO mortgage, repair, and HOA bank feed lines)
    • Uncategorized receipts (Isolates your incoming QBO property management wire transfers)

Once saved, those blue numerical links will stay permanently visible on your cash dashboard screen, giving you a one-click entry point to clean out your bank feeds every single month.

  1. Click on the Bank and Cash Accounts tab on your left sidebar menu.
  2. Look directly at your main Checking Account data row grid.
  3. You will notice two brand-new, plain numeric columns that appeared next to your balances:
    • Uncategorized Receipts
    • Uncategorized Payments
  4. The Access Point: Click directly on the blue numeric digit links inside those columns (e.g., if it says 24, click the number 24). This will instantly open your raw, filtered queue of imported statement lines.

Optional but highly recommended for rental portfolios:

  1. Go to Settings:
    • Check the boxes for:
    • Payments (Displays your total lifetime outgoing transactions)
    • Receipts (Displays your total lifetime incoming revenues)
  2. Click the Update button at the bottom of the screen to commit your grid layout rules.

Path B: Check Your Side Tabs (The Workspace Way)

You can view the transactions here, but not assign them.

  1. Click directly on the Payments tab on your left sidebar menu to see all outgoing money (HOAs, Mortgages).
  2. Click directly on the Receipts tab on your left sidebar menu to see all incoming money (Management wires).

Suspense Account

Because your transactions are currently unassigned, if you look at your main Summary screen, you will notice that your special Suspense account has temporarily spiked into a large balance. This is completely normal! Manager.io dumps unassigned QBO imports into Suspense to keep your balance sheet aligned until you click Edit on the lines and apply the split matrices

2. Step-by-Step Allocation Protocols

Click on your uncategorized transaction list. For each of your specific real estate scenarios, use these exact allocation layouts:

The Split Mortgage Payment

Your mortgage payment leaves your bank as a single lump-sum charge, but it must be broken apart into three distinct destinations on the transaction grid.

Click Edit on the imported transaction row and build this split grid:

Line Account Division Description / Memo Amount
Line 1 Mortgage Loan - Sawtooth Sawtooth Principal Reduction ＄ [Principal Amt]
Line 2 Interest Paid (Expense) Sawtooth Monthly Mortgage Interest ＄ [Interest Amt]
Line 3 Mortgage Escrow Account - Sawtooth Sawtooth Escrow Contribution ＄ [Escrow Deposit]
  • Why this works:
    • Line 1 shrinks your liability balance on the Balance Sheet
    • Line 2 claims your immediate tax deduction on the Profit & Loss
    • Line 3 builds up your liquid asset balance inside your escrow tracking ledger.

The Net Property Management Statement Receipt

Without Purchase Invoice

You do not need to create sales invoices for your monthly property management statements. Instead, you can handle the property management statement as a Split Receipt directly inside your imported bank feed.

See below if you prefer a Sales Document or Invoice approach. I use the Sales Document approach.

When the net wire deposit from your management company hits your bank feed, click Edit and break it down line-by-line using the details from their PDF statement:

Line Account Division Description / Memo Amount (Positive/Negative)
Line 1 Rent Income (Income) Sawtooth Gross Tenant Rent Collected 2500.00 (Positive)
Line 2 Management Fees (Expense) Sawtooth 10% Property Management Fee -250.00 (Negative)
Line 3 Repairs & Maintenance Sawtooth Plumber - Fix leaky faucet -180.00 (Negative)
  • The Magic of the Grid: Once you type your gross income as a positive and your expenses as negatives, Manager.io will add them together. As long as the net mathematical result of the grid matches the exact size of the cash wire that hit your bank, you can hit save. The system will automatically route the gross income and individual expenses into your side-by-side property profit columns perfectly [5.1].

With Purchase Invoice

See also:

  • Set Up Management as a Supplier

If creating an invoice-style record fits your mental model and matches your established workflow, you can absolutely use that method in Manager.io!

To get your data into the system a week before the cash actually moves-while maintaining your side-by-side property tracking columns-you will use a Purchase Invoice (Bill) rather than a Sales Invoice.

Because the management company holds your gross tenant funds, subtracts their fees, and tells you what they owe you, they are acting as a clearing vendor. Logging a Purchase Invoice handles this accounting timing gap flawlessly.

Step 1: Create the Management Company Vendor Profile

  1. Go to the Suppliers tab on your left sidebar menu.
  2. Click New Supplier.
  3. Name: Type your property management group (e.g., Premier Property Management).
  4. Division: Leave this field Blank (as established before, because they handle multiple properties).
  5. Click Create.

Step 2: Record the Monthly Statement (1 Week Before Cash Wire)

The moment you receive your digital monthly PDF statement from your manager, you log it into Manager.io immediately to recognize your income and expenses, regardless of when the bank wire clears:

  1. Go to the Purchase Invoices tab.
  2. Click New Purchase Invoice.
  3. Supplier: Select Premier Property Management.
  4. Invoice Date: Set this to the statement closing date.
  5. Build out your property splits line-by-line using positive numbers for expenses and negative numbers for rent income:
    Row Account Division Description / Memo Amount
    Line 1 Rent Income (Income) Sawtooth Gross Rent Collected -2500.00
    Line 2 Management Fees (Expense) Sawtooth Monthly Management Fee 250.00
    Line 3 Repairs & Maintenance Sawtooth Emergency Plumbing Repair 450.0
    Line 4 Rent Income (Income) Silverbell Gross Rent Collected -2200.00
    Line 5 Management Fees (Expense) Silverbell Monthly Management Fee 220.00
  6. Click Create.

🔍 Reading the Invoice Result (Handling Both Scenarios)

Look closely at the Total figure calculated at the bottom of your saved invoice:

  • Scenario A: A Negative Total (-＄3,780.00): This means they owe you money. Manager.io will automatically place a negative balance under your Accounts Payable ledger, signaling a pending asset wire headed your way.
  • Scenario B: A Positive Total (+＄150.00): This happens if repair expenses exceeded your rent collection. It means you owe them money. It logs a clean liability bill on your dashboard, waiting for you to pay them.

Step 3: Ingesting the QBO File & Matching the Cash

One week later, when your QBO bank file is ingested, that cash transaction will land in your uncategorized feed. Instead of building a complex split grid inside your bank tab, your allocation steps drop to just a single row:

  1. Locate the wire transfer line item inside your bank feed.
  2. Click Edit.
  3. Account: Select Accounts Payable from your dropdown list.
  4. Supplier: Select Premier Property Management.
  5. Invoice: Select your specific backdated monthly statement invoice from the list.
  6. Click Update.

Why This Workflow Is Elegant

The moment you match the bank line to that invoice, your Accounts Payable balance falls back to exactly 0.00.

Your Profit & Loss columns will immediately display your gross rent revenues and isolated management expenses precisely one week before the cash ever touches your checking register, perfectly capturing your preferred accrual-style tracking logic!

3. Streamlining via "Bank Rules" (Next Steps)

You do not have to type these split grids out manually every month. Once you format your first mortgage or HOA payment grid successfully, you can click the New Bank Rule button at the bottom of the transaction screen. You can instruct Manager.io: "Every time an imported line contains the text 'HOA PAY', automatically assign it to the HOA Fees account and lock it to the Sawtooth division."
