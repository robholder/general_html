Billy Tips

Billy's cloak goes through a change in the movie.

  • ExternalLink - Clip - Billy Variants
  • Careful of white shirt vs skin tone.
  • Neck tie is intentionally saturated:
    • Take down value before saturation.
  • Clips in Hair a bit too bright:
    • Fix for shot.
    • The hair clips are not the same material as the earrings.
  • The clothes have a lot of texture:
    • The cloak and scarf is actually constructed of threads in XGen

Alice Tips

  • Alice's cloak is:
    • 'worn'
    • XGen
  • Hose have a sheen/facing ratio (sometimes too bright)

Beef Roger

There are many variants with number of limbs, lack of body etc.

  • He is dirty in terms of design:
    • Makes him difficult to see him.
    • He's often brightened or saturated in Lighting to compensate.
  • Third eye sometimes floats off the head.
  • He has a bite out of his ears.

Mouth Interiors

All chars have the same mouth interior.

  • The tongue gets 'Bubble Gummy' (where it gets too saturated):
    • There is a gradient matte for the tongue

Mattes & Modifiers

CharMattes

Look at the DPix renders of characters to see Mattes:

  • OHIO/Tags/charLightingRef:
    • Scrub through to see the mattes
  • charMatte13:
    • Full costume mattes:
      • Combine R, G + B
  • charMatte17:
    • Interior of cloak and hood
    • Not solid (since it's XGen)
  • charMatte21:
    • Embroidery
    • Designs
    • Detailing
  • charMatte23:
    • Braids & Bangs
  • charMatte9 & charMatte18:
    • Brows & eyes
  • charMatte26:
    • 11 lines and brow shadows
  • charMatte27:
    • Finger Nails (on Billy)

Global Mattes

Should be output automatically by hy_all_utility.

Large general mattes for crowds & characters.

Shimmer Modifier

A modifier for breaking up the specular in skin materials.

  • Nose
  • Cheeks
  • Forehead

Modifier allows for adjustment of specular breakup.

  • Shimmer amount (value)
  • Shimmer scale (size of texture)

To adjust brightness, use:

  • charMatte5 (cheeks)
  • charmatte19 (Tzone: nose and forehead)

Pref

This will be in the hy_all_utility.

Use for moving char tweaks.

  • Currently, Curved cloth XGen doesn't work with Pref.
  • If Pref isn't working on a character check your hy_all_utility to see if the characters are listed.

If not you can replace with a new render pass or replace the enablePref nodes with new ones from Shadowbox
