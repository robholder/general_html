Assign Hotkeys to Shelf Tools

The Ctrl + Alt + Shift + Click Trick

The fastest way to jump straight to any UI element or toolbar button in the Hotkey Manager is using the interface override shortcut:

  • Hold <Ctrl> + <Alt> + <Shift> together on your keyboard.
  • LMB on your custom tool icon sitting in your toolbar shelf.
  • This forces Houdini's Hotkey Manager to open directly and highlight that specific tool, allowing you to click into the hotkey assignment field and press your desired shortcut key.

This method automatically populates the full path address of the tool.
