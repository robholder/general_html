Annual Residential Property Depreciation (27.5-Year Schedule)

Overview

US residential rental buildings are depreciated over 27.5 years using straight-line depreciation and the mid-month convention (a property placed in service at any point during a month receives half a month of depreciation for that first month). Land is never depreciated.

  • Full Year Rate: Depreciable Building Basis ÷ 27.5 (or Basis × 3.636%).
  • Year 1 Mid-Month Formula: (Full Annual Depreciation ÷ 12) × (Remaining Months in Year + 0.5) — e.g., a property placed in service in September gets 3.5 months (Sep 0.5 + Oct + Nov + Dec).
  • Tracking Approach: Keep the original building purchase cost intact on the Balance Sheet and track cumulative write-offs in a separate Accumulated Depreciation contra-asset account (or use Manager.io's built-in Fixed Assets module).

Step 1: Set Up the Chart of Accounts

Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts:

  1. Create the Contra-Asset Account (Balance Sheet side):
    • Click New Account:
      • Name: Accumulated Depreciation - [Property Name]
      • Group: Assets (or your Fixed Assets group)
      • Cash Flow Statement: Investing activities
      • Click Create.
  2. Create the Depreciation Expense Account (Profit & Loss side):
    • Click New Account:
      • Name: Annual Property Depreciation
      • Group: Structural & Non-Cash Deductions (or main Expenses group)
      • Cash Flow Statement: Operating activities
      • Click Create.

Step 2: Record Annual Year-End Depreciation

At year-end (YYYY-12-31), record the write-off using a 2-line Journal Entry (or combine these lines into the same year-end closing entry as your Loan Cost Amortization):

  1. Go to Journal Entries > New Journal Entry.
  2. Date: YYYY-12-31
  3. Narration: Annual Property Depreciation (27.5-Yr) - [Property Name] - YYYY
  4. Check Column - Description below the line items and enter:
    Line Account Description Debit Credit Division
    1 Annual Property Depreciation Annual 27.5-yr building tax deduction [Annual $]   [Property Name]
    2 Accumulated Depreciation - [Property Name] Increases contra-asset offset on BS   [Annual $] [Property Name]
  5. Click Create.

Summary Result: Crediting Accumulated Depreciation - [Property Name] creates a negative asset balance on your Balance Sheet that offsets Building - [Property Name], showing your exact remaining book basis while preserving your original purchase price.
