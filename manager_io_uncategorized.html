Showing Uncategorized Payments

While in the Bank Accounts section and choose Edit Columns and show Uncategorized Payments.

Now that you have the Uncategorized Payments column visible, the entire routine will make perfect sense.

Think of this routine as a two-step holding pattern. Instead of dumping imported transactions directly into your official financial statements, Manager.io drops them into a temporary holding zone (the Uncategorized screen). This gives you a chance to look them over, let your automated rules do the heavy lifting, and approve them before they hit your books. Here is the exact step-by-step routine from importing the CSV file to final approval.

Step 1: Import the CSV File

  1. Go to the Bank and Cash Accounts tab on the left sidebar.
  2. Click the Import Bank Statement button at the top of the page.
  3. Choose your bank account (e.g., your Checking or your Escrow Dummy account).
  4. Click Choose File, select your .csv file, and hit Next.
  5. If prompted to map columns: Ensure Date maps to Date, Description maps to Description, and Amount maps to Amount. Click Import.

Step 2: Access the Holding Zone

Once the import finishes, you will be taken back to your Bank Accounts dashboard.

  1. Look at your bank account row. You will see a blue number (like 1 or 3) sitting inside the Uncategorized Payments column.
  2. Click directly on that blue number. This opens up the holding zone.

Step 3: Let the Rules Run (Understanding the Screen)

When you look at this screen, you are seeing a preview of what your accounting ledger will look like.

  • If a transaction matches your Bank Rules (like our TESTOFFICE rule): Manager.io automatically crosses out the word Suspense and replaces it with your pre-selected account (Office Supplies) and Payee (The Test Vendor). It does this automatically based on your text keywords.
  • If a transaction has NO rule: The account box will just say a plain, un-crossed-out Suspense. This tells you that you need to manually click the dropdown box right there on the screen to choose the correct account or Division before proceeding.

Step 4: The Batch Update (The Final Approval)

The transactions on this screen are not yet officially saved to your business reports. To finalize them:

  1. Look to the far-left side of the rows.
  2. Check the box next to the transactions you want to approve (or check the box at the very top of the column to select all of them at once).
  3. Look at the bottom of your screen and click the blue Batch Update button.

What happens next?

The screen will instantly clear, and the transactions will disappear from the "Uncategorized" list. Manager.io has now officially moved them into your real ledger. If you go look at your Profit & Loss Statement or your Divisions (Properties) reports, the numbers from those transactions will now beautifully appear in your financial data.
