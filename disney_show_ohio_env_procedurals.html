Workflow 1: Editing the Material of Instanced Geometry

Example: Change the color of a candle inside an aurora procedural

  • Add a modifier to your scene: tab → dpipe Modifier
  • Make sure Palette Prim is set to your target material palette (e.g., /shot/shot_modifications)
  • Choose the appropriate modifier template from the modifier's template dropdown
  • Update the values as needed
  • Switch to the Assignments tab of the modifier
  • Before adding assignments, update your viewport selection settings:
    • By default, Houdini selects the top-level asset node when you click in the viewport. To select individual instance geometry, you must change the selection mode.
    • Choose the Select tool from the left side of the viewport.
    • In the toolbar at the top of the viewport, open the dropdown next to Kind and choose Leaf Point Instances.
  • Select the geometry that you want to bind to the modifier
  • Press the Add Patch button

The resulting assignment string will look something like: 

:wdas:shot:hexePortalMuseumDoorEntrance:auroraCandles_node:17:hexeCandleA:candleMelt0001_geo

The number 17 is the aurora instance index, identifying the specific candle instance being modified.
