Manager.io - Summary Options - Accrual vs Cash Basis

Checking "Show balances on cash basis" changes your Summary screen from Accrual accounting to Cash accounting.

It tells Manager.io to ignore unpaid bills and uncollected rent, displaying only the money that has physically moved in or out of your bank accounts.

Cash Basis vs. Accrual Basis on the Summary Screen

Feature Unchecked (Accrual Basis — Default) Checked (Cash Basis)
Rental Income Counts rent the moment you issue a Sales Invoice or charge a tenant, even if they haven't paid yet. Counts rent only on the date the cash actually hits your bank account (Receipt).
Expenses & Bills Counts repairs and management fees the moment you enter a Purchase Invoice, even if it is still unpaid. Counts expenses only on the date you actually pay the bill (Payment).
Receivables & Payables Shows Accounts receivable (unpaid tenant balances) and Accounts payable (unpaid vendor bills) on your Balance Sheet. Hides Accounts receivable and Accounts payable from your Balance Sheet entirely.

Which Setting Should You Use for Rental Properties?

  • Leave it Checked (Cash Basis) for IRS Tax Prep: Most individual real estate investors and small rental LLCs file their Schedule E or Form 1065 tax returns on a cash basis. Checking this box ensures your Summary Profit & Loss matches the exact cash numbers your CPA needs at tax time.
  • Leave it Unchecked (Accrual Basis) for Day-to-Day Tracking: If you enter Property Management Purchase Invoices before paying them and want to see your unpaid vendor bills (Accounts payable) or unpaid tenant rent (Accounts receivable) right on your dashboard, leave it unchecked—and simply generate a Cash Basis Profit & Loss Statement under the Reports tab at year-end.
