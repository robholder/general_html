Material Enabled and Emissive

Video Frame #: 3034

(Material) Enabled

(Use with caution. Not normal workflow.)

This removes the light from the render.

  • material:enabled is light enabled basically:
    • Sometimes shows up as correctly as enabled due to some bug.
    • This is linked to the Enabled checkbox on the light.

Emissive

This does not remove the light from the render, although it may well render 'black'. It will still occupy a 'category'.

  • Emissive:
    • This parameter toggles Emmisive checkbox on the light.

Customization of UI

  • Rearrange columns:
    • Tricky you have to follow the divider down (?) NOT TRIED YET
  • Change width of columns:
    • you can re-space the columns by grabbing the division between them.

Choose Columns

  • RMB in the title area:
    • Choose Columns:
      • check boxes
      • There's an Edit Attribute Columns at the base:
        • you can filter with search string here
        • If you are looking for a parm, hover the mouse over a parm panel to see programmatic names

Saving Settings

  • RMB in the title area:
    • Save Changes
    • Save As...
    • Rename
    • Revert Changes

Light Management

  • You can re-order the lights

However...

  • Inherited Lights:
    • You can't move between 'palettes'
  • Local Lights (created in the particular Lightbox):
    • You CAN move between 'palettes'
  • Palettes stay where they are.

Remembering States

  • Dedicated Lightbox Panel may not remember open/close states
  • Parameter view of Lightbox is more robust at this.

Duplicating Lights

  • RMB > Duplicate:
    • you get a dialog to name/rename
    • Duplicate and Cancel buttons.

Solo

  • This parameter effects Viewport and Ion
  • Does not pass through to Helios and final renders
  • RMB to clear all solo selections
