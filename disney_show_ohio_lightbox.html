Material Enabled and Emissive

Video Frame #: 3034

(Material) Enabled

(Use with caution. Not normal workflow.)

This removes the light from the render.

  • material:enabled is light enabled basically:
    • Sometimes shows up as correctly as enabled due to some bug.
    • This is linked to the Enabled checkbox on the light.

Emissive

This does not remove the light from the render, although it may well render 'black'. It will still occupy a 'category'.

  • Emissive:
    • This parameter toggles Emmisive checkbox on the light.
