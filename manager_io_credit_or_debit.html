Manager.io - Credit or Debit?

In double-entry accounting, equity accounts (including your capital investments) always increase with a credit balance. Entering it as a credit tells the system that you have injected capital into the business, which will offset your mortgage liabilities and turn your equity numbers positive.

Summary of Opening Balance Rules for Your Setup

As you finalize your migration from QuickBooks into Manager.io, you can use this quick reference sheet to ensure every type of account is entered with the correct accounting behavior:

  • Cash / Escrow Bank Accounts: Entered as a Debit (Increases your active cash assets).
  • Property Land Value: Entered as a Debit (Increases your real estate land assets).
  • Fixed Assets (Building Acquisition Cost): Entered as a positive number (Acts as an asset Debit).
  • Fixed Assets (Accumulated Depreciation): Entered as a positive number (Acts as an asset Credit reduction).
  • Mortgage Loan Accounts (Journal Entry): Entered as a Credit (Increases your liabilities/debts).
  • Owner's Capital Accounts (Investment): Entered as a Credit (Increases your business equity).
