Exporting CSV (TSV) Data

Manager.io allows you to export almost any account ledger, transaction list, or financial report directly to a TSV (Tab-Separated Values) file or copy it straight into a spreadsheet.

While Manager.io labels its export button "Copy to clipboard" rather than "Export CSV," pasting that output into Excel, LibreOffice Calc, or Google Sheets cleanly separates every column—or you can save it directly as a .csv file.

Method 1: Export a Single Account's Transaction Ledger (Best for Isolating One Account)

If you want to isolate all transactions for a specific expense, asset, or capital account:

  1. Click on the Summary tab on your left sidebar menu.
  2. Click the blue numerical balance next to the specific account you want to isolate (e.g., Repairs & Maintenance, Partner A - Capital, or Retained earnings).
  3. (Optional) Use the Search box at the top right to filter by a specific property division, keyword, or year.
  4. Scroll to the very bottom-right corner of the transaction table and click the Copy to clipboard button.
  5. Open your spreadsheet program (Excel, LibreOffice Calc, or Google Sheets), click cell A1, and press Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on Mac). Every column (Date, Reference, Description, Account, Amount, Balance) will populate cleanly into separate cells.

Method 2: Export an Entire Tab (Receipts, Payments, Invoices, or Journal Entries)

If you want a spreadsheet of all bank receipts, vendor bills, or journal entries:

  1. Click on the desired tab on your left sidebar (such as Receipts, Payments, Purchase Invoices, or Journal Entries).
  2. (Optional) Use the Search filter or sort by clicking any column header.
  3. Scroll to the bottom-right corner and click Copy to clipboard.
  4. Paste directly into cell A1 of your spreadsheet, then save the file as .csv (File > Save As > CSV).

Method 3: Export Formatted Financial Reports (General Ledger Transactions Report)

If you want a multi-account breakdown grouped cleanly by account name and date range:

  1. Go to the Reports tab on your left sidebar menu.
  2. Under General Ledger, click General Ledger Transactions (or General Ledger Summary).
  3. Click New Report
  4. Set your From and To dates (and optionally filter by a specific Division)
  5. Click Create.
  6. Click the Copy to clipboard button at the top of the generated report and paste it into your spreadsheet.

Quick Tip for Command-Line / Vim Users

If you prefer creating a .csv file directly from your terminal without opening a spreadsheet GUI first, click Copy to clipboard in Manager.io and run this one-liner in your terminal to convert the tab-separated clipboard output straight into a standard comma-separated file:

  • Linux (xclip): xclip -o -selection clipboard | tr '\t' ',' > export.csv
  • macOS (pbpaste): pbpaste | tr '\t' ',' > export.csv
