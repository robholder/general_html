Manager.io - Using 'Faked' CSV Imports

UNTESTED AI SUGGESTION

The Technique: Bulk-Automating Escrow Payments

Step 1: Create your Multi-Line Payment Rule

First, build the rule that handles the logic.

  1. Go to Settings > Bank Rules > Payment Rules > New Payment Rule.
  2. If description contains: Set to County Tax Payment.
  3. Lines Section: Click Add Line so you have your two balanced rows: • Line 1 Account: Property Taxes (Expense) | Division: Property A • Line 2 Account: Escrow Account - Property A (Asset) | Division: Property A
  4. Leave the amount fields completely blank. (Manager.io will automatically copy the imported transaction amount into the first line, and you will just copy it into the second line as a negative number when reviewing).

Step 2: Create a Simple Text File (The "Fake QBO" Shortcut)

Instead of typing the transaction into the software manually, you write it in a plain text file on your computer notes app. Save it as a .csv file (Manager.io accepts CSV imports for bank statements). Make a spreadsheet or text file that looks like this: 

Date,Description,Amount
2026-11-01,County Tax Payment,2500.00

Step 3: The Magic Import

  1. Go to Bank and Cash Accounts and click on your Escrow Clearing (Dummy) account.
  2. Click Import Bank Statement and upload your tiny CSV file.
  3. The Result: Because it went through the import engine, Manager.io's Payment Rule instantly fires.
  4. On your review screen, you will see the transaction already split into the Property Taxes expense and the Escrow Account asset, with your Division (Property) pre-tagged.
  5. You simply change the second line's amount to a negative (-2500.00) so it nets to zero, and hit approve.

Summary

If you type it directly on the screen, you must use the Clone button on a past transaction to get pre-filled lines. If you want the Payment Rules engine to build the lines for you, you must pass the transaction through the Import Bank Statement button using a quick CSV spreadsheet line.

Here is copy-and-paste CSV template designed to import multiple property tax payments simultaneously.

Multi-Property Tax CSV Template

To use this, open any basic text editor (like Notepad or TextEdit), paste the block below, update the text to match your real property details, and save the file with a .csv extension (e.g., escrow_taxes.csv). 

Date,Description,Amount
2026-11-01,County Tax Payment - 123 Main St,0.00
2026-11-01,County Tax Payment - 456 Oak Ave,0.00
2026-11-01,County Tax Payment - 789 Pine Rd,0.00

Why the Amount in the CSV Must Be 0.00

You asked why you shouldn't use -2500.00 (instead of 2500.00) in the CSV file.

Because we are recording an escrow payment inside Manager.io, the net cash impact on your bank account is zero. If you put 2500.00 or -2500.00 in the CSV file, Manager.io will believe that your bank account balance physically increased or decreased by that amount.

By setting the CSV amount to 0.00 or a positive value, you achieve three critical things:

  1. Protects your cash balance: It forces the final transaction total to remain $0.00, keeping your ledger accurate.
  2. Triggers your bank rule: The text County Tax Payment still triggers your automated split lines during the import.
  3. Allows manual line entry: When the transaction imports, you simply type the actual tax amount into the pre-formatted lines during your quick review step.

How to Configure Your Rules for the Multi-Property CSV

To make this template work perfectly for multiple houses at once, you will create a unique Payment Rule for each property address using the specific text from your CSV description.

Rule for Property 1:

  • If description contains: County Tax Payment - 123 Main St
    • Line 1 Account: Property Taxes | Division: 123 Main St
    • Line 2 Account: Escrow Account - 123 Main St | Division: 123 Main St

Rule for Property 2:

  • If description contains: County Tax Payment - 456 Oak Ave
    • Line 1 Account: Property Taxes | Division: 456 Oak Ave
    • Line 2 Account: Escrow Account - 456 Oak Ave | Division: 456 Oak Ave

When you import the 0.00 CSV file, Manager.io will read the description lines, instantly build the two correct account rows for the matching property, and hand you a clean screen where you only need to type the positive and negative tax figures for the year.

Import Historical Data

To set up your historical mortgage balances in bulk using a CSV import, we will use a similar zero-cash method.

When importing historical balances (also called opening balances), we want to establish your loan liabilities and escrow assets on your Balance Sheet without making it look like cash physically moved in or out of your actual checking account on that day.

Here is the exact CSV template and the matching account setup to get your portfolio initialized in Manager.io.

Multi-Property Mortgage Setup CSV Template

Open a text editor (like Notepad), paste this block, replace the numbers and names with your actual property data, and save it as a .csv file (e.g., mortgage_setup.csv). 

Date,Description,Amount
2026-11-01,Initial Mortgage Setup - 123 Main St,0.00
2026-11-01,Initial Mortgage Setup - 456 Oak Ave,0.00

Step 1: Create the Balancing Account

Because opening balances require an accounting offset, you need a temporary landing place for the equity side of the transaction.

  1. Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts.
  2. Under the Equity section, look for or create an account called Capital Accounts or Owner's Equity / Retained Earnings.

Step 2: Set Up the Payment Rules (One per Property)

Before importing the CSV, create a unique Payment Rule for each property description so Manager.io knows exactly how to split the lines.

Rule for Property 1:

  • If description contains: Initial Mortgage Setup - 123 Main St
    • Line 1 Account: Mortgage Loan - 123 Main St (Liability) | Division: 123 Main St
    • Line 2 Account: Escrow Account - 123 Main St (Asset) | Division: 123 Main St
    • Line 3 Account: Capital Accounts or Owner's Equity (Equity) | Division: 123 Main St

Step 3: Import and Enter the Balances

  1. Go to Bank and Cash Accounts, select your Escrow Clearing (Dummy) account, and click Import Bank Statement to upload your CSV file.
  2. Manager.io will read the description, match your rule, and present you with the 3 pre-formatted lines for each property.
  3. Now, enter your real historical figures using this specific math to ensure the transaction equals 0.00:
    Line Account Type Real Example Numbers Direction
    Mortgage Loan Liability 300,000.00 Positive (Increases what you owe)
    Escrow Account Asset -5,000.00 Negative (Establishes your bank savings)
    Owner's Equity Equity -295,000.00 Negative (Balances the net liability out to zero)
    Total Transaction - 0.00 (Matches your CSV line)

Why this works perfectly:

Once you hit approve, your Balance Sheet will instantly show that you owe \(300,000 on the home, have \)5,000 sitting in your escrow account, and your personal equity has been adjusted to absorb the net property liability—all without touching your real-world checking account data.
