2. Control Accounts & Sub-Ledger Modules

Control Accounts act as summary containers on the Balance Sheet to group individual sub-accounts into clean, single-line items.

We need Control Accounts for the Mortgage Escrows because they are functional bank accounts that receive deposits and write payments.

In Manager.io, a bank account cannot be assigned directly to a standard visual Group. It requires a Control Account to act as its specialized data bridge, linking the banking system to the Balance Sheet.

Other entities (like basic expense or asset rows) are just passive text lines. They do not process live bank transactions or cut checks, so they only need a visual Group folder to stay organized.

NOTE: There is a second strategy below this example. Choose whether you want to see a 'total' in the summary or all Escrow accounts listed individually. If you only have one property, strategy one if fine.

Strategy 1

Step A: Create the "Property Escrow Buckets" (Mortgage Escrow) Control Account (Assets)

  1. Go to Settings > Control Accounts.
  2. Select Bank and Cash Accounts
  3. Click the New Control Account button.
  4. Configure the form:
    • Name: Mortgage Escrow
    • Type: Select Asset.
  5. Click Create.

Step B: Create and Link Real Escrow Cash Accounts

Escrow accounts hold cash belonging to you in trust. They are assets and must be created as Cash Accounts so they can receive multi-line statement file imports.

  1. Go to the Bank and Cash Accounts tab.
  2. Click New Bank or Cash Account.
  3. Build the property account if only one property, and separate property accounts if you have multiple: e.g.,
    • Mortgage Escrow Account - Sawtooth and Mortgage Escrow Account - Silverbell
    • Control Account: Select Mortgage Escrow.
    • Division: Select the corresponding property division (e.g., Sawtooth and Silverbell).
  4. Click Create.

Strategy 2

Alternative Structure for Mortgage Escrows Control Account

In Manager.io's engine, a Control Account is hardcoded to do exactly one thing on the Summary dashboard: collapse everything into a single line item. Subsidiary accounts nested inside a control account are explicitly hidden from the main view unless you physically click on the blue balance to drill into them.

To see Mortgage Escrow - Sawtooth and Mortgage Escrow - Silverbell listed as separate, live rows on your main Summary screen, you must change how they are built. You achieve this using the Individual Control Accounts Strategy:

The Procedure to Show Both Running Totals

Instead of using one parent control account container for both properties, you create a distinct Custom Control Account for each individual property escrow and group them using a standard visual heading folder.

Step pre-A (If needed): Delete the Combined Parent Control Account

If you already have a combined control account and need to delete it:

  1. Go to Bank and Cash Accounts
  2. Click Edit on each Mortgage Escrow account in turn and delete the Control Account entry.
  3. Go to Settings > Control Accounts > Bank and Cash Accounts.
  4. Select the Edit button on your combined Mortgage Escrows control account
  5. Delete it. (Don't worry, this won't break your bank accounts; it just sets them back to the default cash category).

Step A: Create One, Two or More Independent Control Accounts

Example for two properties: Sawtooth & Silverbell:

  1. In Settings > Control Accounts > Bank and Cash Accounts
  2. For each property:
    • Click New Control Account.
    • Name: Mortgage Escrow - Sawtooth (omit Property label if only one property).
    • Group: Assets.
  3. Click Create.
  4. Click New Control Account again for any further properties.
    • Name: Mortgage Escrow - Silverbell
    • Group: Assets.
  5. Click Create.

Step B: Create and Link Real Escrow Cash Accounts

Escrow accounts hold cash belonging to you in trust. They are assets and must be created as Cash Accounts so they can receive multi-line statement file imports.

  1. Go to the Bank and Cash Accounts tab.
  2. Click New Bank or Cash Account.
  3. Build the separate property accounts: e.g.,
    • Mortgage Escrow Account - Sawtooth and Mortgage Escrow Account - Silverbell
    • Control Account: Select Mortgage Escrow (or Mortgage Escrow - Sawtooth if you have multiple properties).
    • Division: Select the corresponding property division (e.g., Sawtooth and Silverbell).
  4. Click Create.

Step C: Clean Up the Look with a Group Folder

Now that they are separate control accounts, they will show up individually on your Summary page. To make them look like nested child elements under a tidy header:

  1. Go to Settings > Chart of Accounts.
  2. Click New Group on the Balance Sheet (Assets) side.
  3. Name: type Mortgage Escrows.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Locate your new Mortgage Escrow - Sawtooth and Mortgage Escrow - Silverbell rows on your Chart of Accounts layout grid.
  6. Click Edit for each in turn.
  7. Use the dropdown edit fields to change their Group folder path to your brand-new Mortgage Escrows heading folder.
